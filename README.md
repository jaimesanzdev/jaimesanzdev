# Jaime Sanz - Mathematics × Computer Science × Game Development

## 🧠 Who am I?

I am an advanced Mathematics and Computer Science student (University of Murcia & SDSU) with a strong analytical foundation and a deep interest in game development.

I enjoy working at both levels of a project: defining and refining design decisions, and then carefully implementing those ideas into clean, robust systems. For me, good development is not only about making things work, it is about understanding why they work.

I value doing things properly. I am naturally analytical, I like to question assumptions, and I prefer validating decisions instead of following conventions blindly. Whether it is a gameplay mechanic, a system architecture choice, or a technical implementation, I aim for coherence, clarity and long-term quality.
